KMITORA REFERENCE MIGRATION 001

SOURCE: F1 Legacy CRM
TARGET: F2 Modern CRM

BUSINESS RULES

BR-001 Active customers shall be eligible for migration.
BR-002 Duplicate customers shall be identified using normalized email and phone.
BR-003 IN, IND and India shall normalize to INDIA.
BR-004 A and ACTIVE shall normalize to ACTIVE.
BR-005 Negative credit limits shall be rejected.
BR-006 Invalid email addresses shall create a data-quality exception.
BR-007 Unknown customer status shall require review.
BR-008 Every order must reference an existing migrated customer.
BR-009 COMPLETE and COMPLETED shall normalize to COMPLETED.
BR-010 Negative order amounts shall be rejected.
BR-011 Missing mandatory order dates shall be quarantined for review.
BR-012 No rejected record may silently enter F2.
BR-013 Every migration decision must generate traceable evidence.
BR-014 Source records must remain unchanged.
BR-015 Production activation is prohibited during this reference test.
